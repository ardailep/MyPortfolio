Who am I?

A sculptor's eye, translated into polygons.

I am currently studying Sculpture at Anadolu University, building a strong foundation in form, volume, composition, and visual language. This shapes both my physical work and my digital production.

On the digital side, I primarily focus on 3D modeling and visual production. I create stylized, hand-paint–oriented models for games and interactive projects.

I work mainly with Blender and Roblox and have a working knowledge of Luau to support the production process.
