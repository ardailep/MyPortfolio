Who am I?

Hi! I make 3D models and animations for games.

I'm 20, and I'm a designer, modeler and animator. I've been making 3D assets, environments and character animations for games and digital projects for several years now.

I'm also studying Sculpture at Anadolu University, so I work on physical pieces as well as digital ones.

I mostly go for a stylized, hand-painted look. I usually work in Blender and Roblox Studio, and I know enough Luau to script my own things in Roblox.
